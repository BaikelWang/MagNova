# Notices

MagNova integrates with, but does not redistribute, upstream projects and model assets including MiniMind, MatterGen, and MatterSim. Users must obtain those projects and assets from their official sources and comply with their respective licenses and usage terms.

The `training/minimind` utilities expect a compatible MiniMind checkout. The `integrations/mattergen` workflow expects a compatible MatterGen checkout and separately downloaded checkpoints/reference data.

No SACGT implementation or model asset is included in this public repository.
